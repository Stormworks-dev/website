Vehicle Optimizer

Why?

I had some ideas on how to make Stormworks vehicles less laggy, and this project is what came out of that. The first and currently only tool, deAdditizer, is pretty simple, but still works well. I have more ideas for tools on the way, and i'll keep building them as I have time to get them working.

For now:

You can check out the releases for update notes, browse the repo on GitHub, or join the Discord.
