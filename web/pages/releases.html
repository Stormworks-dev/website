v0.1.0 - The beginning

August 21, 2026

Welcome to the first pre-release of Stormworks.dev.

This is the foundation: the site, the first tool, and a few other little things. The first tool, deAdditizer, is a lightweight XML parser for reducing Stormworks vehicle file sizes. I'd like to say it's anything more than a proof of concept, but at the moment it's slower than I ever would've expected. At least for now, I think it looks cool.

Thank you for being here.

You can expect a full parser rework, cool stats, and prettier pages soon enough.
